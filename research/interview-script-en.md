# Interview script — Swiss home-buying journey (EN) · lean 30-min version

**Rules:** One question at a time, then silence. Never suggest answers or pitch features. Follow their story. Past behavior > hypotheticals. Couples: "and for you — same or different?" Don't mention Plinthly.

**Structure: 6 core questions. Everything else is a probe you use only when their story touches it.**

---

## Open (0–2 min)

> Thanks for the time. I'm researching how people in Switzerland approach buying property — your real experience, no right answers, nothing being sold. ~30 minutes, confidential, anonymized. OK to record?

☐ Consent ☐ Recording

## Core questions

**Q1 — Context + trigger (2–5 min)**
Where are you with the topic of buying property — and was there a moment it got serious? What happened?

**Q2 — Journey walkthrough (5–20 min) ← spend half the session here**
Walk me through it step by step from that moment, like a story. What was the very first thing you actually did?

*Keep the story moving with only these:*
- What exactly did you do / use? Who did you talk to?
- How did that feel?
- What came next — why that?
- *(Must cover before leaving Q2):* How did you figure out what you could afford? Did you trust the number? · Where did things stall or stop?

*Segment B (bought already) — same question, past tense, and make sure the story reaches:* the offer, choosing/negotiating the bank, a moment it almost fell apart, what surprised you only after it was too late, what you'd do differently.

**Q3 — Hardest decision (20–22 min)**
Across the whole process — what's the single hardest decision? What makes it hard?

**Q4 — Trust (22–24 min)**
Whose information do you trust in this process? Whose not? Why?

**Q5 — Pre-bank wish (24–26 min)**
Before (you first talked / people first talk) to a bank: what do you wish you could know, check, or prepare at that point?

**Q6 — Magic wand (26–28 min)**
One thing about this whole process gets fixed overnight — what do you pick?

## Close (28–30 min)

What should I have asked that I didn't? → Thank you.

---

## Probe bank — only if their story opens the door

- **Pension money:** When did you learn you could use Pillar 2/3? How did you feel about using it?
- **Learning network:** Who taught you that? (colleagues / family / tools)
- **Rent vs. buy:** Did you ever compare financially? How?
- **Time-to-goal:** Did you try to work out *when* you could afford what you want, not just whether now?
- **Energy/renovation (gated — only if THEY raise condition, heating, renovation, costs):** Did you put numbers on it? Subsidies? *If never raised, that's the finding — don't introduce it.*

## If running over
Cut in this order: probe bank → Q5 → Q4. Never cut Q2 or Q6.

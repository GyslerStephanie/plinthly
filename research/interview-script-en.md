# Interview script — Swiss home-buying journey (EN)

30 min · semi-structured · foundational
**Moderator rules:** Ask, then shut up — let silence work. Never suggest an answer. Follow their story, not the script order. Past behavior > hypotheticals ("what did you do" beats "what would you do"). Don't mention Plinthly until Block 6.

---

## Block 1 — Intro & consent (0–3 min)

> Hi, thanks for taking the time. I'm doing research on how people in Switzerland approach buying property. I'm interested in your real experience — there are no right or wrong answers, and you can't say anything wrong.
>
> This takes about 30 minutes. Everything you share stays confidential and is only used in anonymized form. Is it OK if I record this so I don't have to scribble notes the whole time? You can ask me to stop at any point.

☐ Consent given ☐ Recording started

> One thing before we start: I'm not selling anything and I'm not testing *you*. I want to learn how this process really works for people.

## Block 2 — Context (3–7 min)

1. To start: how do you live right now — renting, owning, something else?
2. Where would you say you are with the topic of buying property? *(just thinking / actively looking / made offers / bought / renovating)*
3. **Trigger:** Do you remember the moment it became serious — when "maybe someday" turned into "let's actually look into this"? What happened?
   - *Probe:* Was there a specific event? (rent increase, kid, inheritance, a friend buying…)

## Block 3 — Journey walkthrough (7–18 min) ← core of the session

> I'd like you to walk me through your journey step by step, like a story. Start from that first serious moment.

4. **What was the very first thing you actually did?**
   - *Probes per step (repeat as the story unfolds):*
     - What exactly did you do / look at / use?
     - Who did you talk to?
     - How did you feel at that point?
     - What did you do next — and why that next?
5. *(For prospective buyers who haven't acted much yet):* What do you *think* the steps ahead of you are? Which one comes first?
6. **Money moment:** At what point did you try to figure out what you could afford? How did you do it?
   - *Probe:* Which tool/calculator/person? Did you trust the number? Why / why not?
7. **Stall points:** Was there a point where things stalled or you stopped? What exactly stopped you?
8. **Knowledge gaps:** Was there a moment you realized you didn't understand something important? What was it? *(listen for: 20% rule, 5% stress test, Pillar 2, taxes, Eigenmietwert…)* How did you fill that gap?

*Moderator: capture steps verbatim in note template — these build the journey map.*

## Block 4 — Decisions, needs & trust (18–25 min)

9. Looking at the whole process: what's the **single hardest decision** in it for you? What makes it hard?
10. Whose information do you **trust** in this process? Whose do you not? Why?
    - *Probe:* Bank advisors? Comparison portals? Friends? Why the difference?
11. *(Only if not yet mentioned)* Did the **condition or energy side** of a property — heating, renovation needs, energy costs — play any role in your thinking? How?
    - *Probe if yes:* Did you ever try to put numbers on it? Subsidies?
    - *Do not lead — if it never came up, that IS the finding.*
12. Imagine the step **before you first talk to a bank**. What do you wish you could know, check, or prepare at that point?

## Block 5 — Magic wand & wrap (25–28 min)

13. Magic wand: one thing about this whole process gets fixed or removed overnight — what do you choose?
14. What should I have asked you about this that I didn't?

> Thank you — this was exactly what I needed.

## Block 6 — OPTIONAL concept reaction (28–30 min, only if ahead of schedule)

> Before we finish — I'm working on a free, no-signup tool for exactly this pre-bank phase. May I show you the start page for one minute? I only want your first impression, brutally honest.

*Show Plinthly landing. Do NOT explain it first.*

15. What do you think this is? Would it have a place in the journey you just described — where?
16. First gut reaction: anything you'd distrust here?

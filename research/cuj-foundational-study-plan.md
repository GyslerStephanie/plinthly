# Plinthly — Foundational CUJ Study Plan

**Study type:** Foundational / generative (not usability). Goal: understand the real-world journey of buying property in Switzerland — steps, decisions, needs, anxieties — before designing against it.
**Date:** June 2026 · **Owner:** Steph

---

## 1. Background

Plinthly assumes a 4-phase journey (Can I buy? → What to look for → My real options → Action plan). That model was built from regulation and product intuition, not observed behavior. This study grounds it: what do prospective and recent Swiss home buyers *actually* do, in what order, and where do they struggle?

## 2. Objectives

1. Map the critical steps people take (or expect to take) when buying property in Switzerland — the actual journey, not the idealized one.
2. Identify the hardest decisions, biggest information gaps, and emotional low points.
3. Understand information sources and trust: who do they listen to, who do they distrust, and why.
4. Learn what role sustainability/energy considerations play (if any) in affordability and choice.
5. Surface unmet needs: what would they *like* to be able to do before talking to a bank?

## 3. Research questions

- RQ1: What triggers the decision to seriously consider buying?
- RQ2: What steps do people take, in what sequence? Which steps are shared across participants (the critical path) vs. idiosyncratic?
- RQ3: Where do journeys stall or end? What's the binding constraint as *they* perceive it (money, knowledge, market, courage)?
- RQ4: Which tools/sources do they use today (bank calculators, portals, friends, advisors)? What do they trust?
- RQ5: Do energy class, renovation cost, or subsidies enter the picture? When and how?
- RQ6: What would ideal pre-bank support look like?

**Explicitly out of scope:** screen-level usability of Plinthly. (Optional 5-min concept-reaction module exists in the script for sessions that run fast — first impressions only, no tasks.)

## 4. Method

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | Semi-structured 1:1 interview, journey-walkthrough technique |
| Duration | 30 min |
| Mode | Remote (video) or in person |
| Languages | EN or DE — participant's choice; scripts mirrored |
| Sample | 6–8 participants (5+ per segment lens is ideal; with n=6–8 treat segments as lenses, not cells) |
| Recording | With consent; notes otherwise |

## 5. Participants & screener

**Segments**
- A. Prospective first-time buyers (CH): actively thinking about buying in next 0–3 years, haven't bought yet.
- B. Recent buyers / renovators: bought in last ~3 years or planning/doing an energy retrofit.
- Convenience sample (friends/acquaintances) OK — but screen them; exclude anyone who has seen Plinthly or knows the product details (you can still interview them, just flag the data).

**Screener questions**
1. Do you currently live in Switzerland? (must: yes)
2. Have you seriously considered buying property in Switzerland, or bought in the last 3 years? (must: yes; "seriously" = took at least one concrete step: looked at listings with intent, used a calculator, talked to anyone about financing)
3. Where are you in the process? (thinking / actively looking / made offers / bought / renovating)
4. Are you the person (or one of the people) making the financial decision? (must: yes)
5. Do you work in banking, real estate, or mortgage brokerage? (exclude)
6. Preferred interview language? (EN/DE)

**Quota target:** ≥3 from segment A, ≥2 from segment B, mix of cantons/language regions if possible.

## 6. Session structure (30 min)

| Min | Block | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Intro & consent | Rapport, recording consent, "no right answers" |
| 3–7 | Context | Living situation, where they are in the journey, trigger |
| 7–18 | Journey walkthrough | The core: step-by-step reconstruction of their actual/planned path |
| 18–25 | Decisions, needs & trust | Hardest decision, info gaps, trusted sources, sustainability probe |
| 25–28 | Magic wand + wrap | Ideal support; anything missed |
| 28–30 | (Optional) concept reaction | Only if ahead of time: show Plinthly landing, first impressions |

## 7. Analysis plan

1. After each session: 10-min debrief note — top 3 observations, surprising quotes, journey steps mentioned (use note template in scripts).
2. After all sessions: affinity-map observations into themes per RQ.
3. Build a **consolidated journey map**: steps on x-axis (only steps mentioned by ≥3 participants make the critical path), per step: actions, sources used, emotions, pain points, quotes.
4. Compare resulting map against Plinthly's 4-phase model — where it confirms, contradicts, or extends.
5. Output: 1-page insight summary + journey map + prioritized opportunity list (impact × confidence).

## 8. Risks / honesty notes

- n=6–8 convenience-skewed sample → directional, not statistical. Don't over-generalize across language regions.
- Friends will be polite. The script avoids mentioning Plinthly until the very end for this reason — journey data stays clean even if concept feedback is flattering.
- Recent buyers have hindsight bias; prospective buyers have speculation bias. Mark each data point with segment.

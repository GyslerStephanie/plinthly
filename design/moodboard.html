01

Where Plinthly actually is

The honest read: Plinthly is not in the generic AI state. A prior pass already did the hard, high-leverage work — a real Satoshi/Inter pairing, a functional px type scale, a monochrome neutral ramp where colour is functional not decorative, no colour fills larger than a badge, and a genuine signature move on the gap chart (diagonal hatch fills, no red "you failed" bars).

The real risk is the opposite problem: monochrome-austere has its own generic register — the Linear / Vercel minimalist clone. Restraint without a point of view reads as absence, not maturity.

So "less generic" here means adding intentional character — warmth, one confident accent, texture, a typographic stance — not stripping more away.

02

Brand north star

Plinthly is the neutral pre-step — the trustworthy minute before the bank, the broker, the builder.

A

Trust over persuasion

It must look like a public utility or a notary, not a lead-gen funnel. Calm, factual, never pushy. The design's job is to be believed.

B

The number is the hero

CHF figures, ranges and gaps are the product. Typography, tabular alignment and chart language should make the numbers the most beautiful, legible thing on screen.

C

Swiss, and sustainable

Credible claim to Swiss design heritage (grotesques, grid, restraint) plus a material, earthy register that signals the sustainability lens without going "eco-green-cliché".

04

From moodboard to system — the Thumbprint discipline

Thumbtack's Thumbprint is the reference for what turns a look into a system. Three things to steal: tokens as a single source of truth with strict naming, a deliberately small number of type options, and principles written down before pixels. Whatever direction wins, it should land as named tokens — not screens.

Token

Single source of truth

Colour, type, spacing, radii stored once as named objects, transformed per platform. Change in one place, propagate everywhere.

Type

A small menu

Thumbprint: 1 brand font, 2 weights, ~8 titles + 3 text sizes. Constraint is the point. Plinthly already has this shape.

Icon

One family only

Pick Lucide (already available) or Phosphor and never mix. Consistent stroke, corner, and metrics across every glyph.

Motion

Documented, not ad-hoc

Define the hover/focus/transition curves and durations once as tokens so micro-interactions feel like one hand made them.

// One token, transformed per platform — the discipline that makes it a system { "name": "Clay", "id": "plinthly-color__accent", "value": "#BD6A45", "type": "color" } CSS → --plinthly-color__accent: #BD6A45; (Tailwind v4 @theme, already your setup) React→ plinthlyColorAccent = "#BD6A45";

Plinthly's head start: your index.css already uses Tailwind v4 @theme tokens with semantic names (--color-ink, --color-positive). Adopting a direction = swapping values + adding an accent + a texture layer + a motion scale. The architecture is already correct.

05

The micro-details that read as "human"

Focus & hover

A 2px accent ring with a soft offset, not the browser default. Hover transitions on a documented curve (≈150ms ease-out), never the Tailwind reflex. These two things alone separate "shipped" from "considered".

Radius logic

Not 8px on everything by reflex. A small scale with intent: inputs & pills share one radius, cards a larger one, the number card sharper or softer to signal hierarchy.

Favicon & mark

Today's favicon is a teal house — it predates the monochrome system and will clash with any chosen accent. The wordmark "Plinthly" set in the display face is the logo; refresh the mark to match.

Microcopy voice

"No selling · No sign-up · Just honest numbers" is already sharp. Keep that register everywhere — specific, plain, slightly opinionated. Generic copy is as much an AI tell as a gradient.

Number presentation

Tabular numerals everywhere (already in use), CHF grouping, and a consistent range syntax ("820k – 1.04M ≈ 920k"). This is the signature surface — invest the most polish here.

Texture, used sparingly

A 3–4% grain on the paper ground and hairline rules add the "made by a person" quality. The discipline is restraint — texture should be felt, not seen.

My read, for you to push on

Start from Alpine Paper, borrow the numeral-as-hero from Editorial.

It's the lowest-risk, highest-payoff move: keeps everything good about the current restraint, adds the warmth and the single confident accent that "honest, trustworthy, Swiss" needs, and leaves room to bring in slate-green only on the sustainability moments. Material Warmth is the more ambitious, more ownable end state if you want to invest in maintaining Fraunces well — a strong "phase two".
